math.woff2: Libertinus Math
Copyright 2012-2024 The Libertinus Project Authors.
https://github.com/alerque/libertinus
Local MathML adjustment: the MATH table's horizontal construction for
tildecomb (U+0303) is also registered for tilde (U+02DC), which browsers
recognize as a horizontal stretchy operator. Its first variant is tilde
(advance 292); the existing tildecomb.size1 through size5 variants are reused.
Glyph outlines and font names are unchanged.
